## Findings

| # | severity | location | finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | low | `africot/planner_cem.py:78,124-130` | `waiting_danfos` in the cost is approximated as predicted queue x the current observed danfo share of the queue, because the world model (SPEC 5.6) does not predict danfos. This is a reasonable reading of the spec, but it is an interpretation the documenter should state, ideally with a line in `DEVIATIONS.md`. |
| 2 | low | `africot/planner_cem.py:100-109,199-203` | `reset()` does not clear the heuristic's arrival-rate estimate `_arr`, so a reused `africot_nowm` controller would carry it into the next episode. No effect on the committed results: `run_experiment.run` builds a new controller for every run. |
| 3 | info | `africot/planner_cem.py:60-84` | The cost is summed per plan step, not weighted by step duration, so plans of long greens are not penalised for the extra time they cover. Matches the spec wording ("summed over the horizon"); worth a sentence in the limitations. |
| 4 | info | `africot/planner_cem.py:40` | The nowm discharge rates (west 1.0, east 0.5 veh/s in phase A; east 1.0 in B; south 0.5 in C) are fixed engineering guesses, not tuned. Fine for an ablation; the write-up should say so. |
