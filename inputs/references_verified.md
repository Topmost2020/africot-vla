**Conventions used**
- **CoRL papers (PMLR).** PMLR prints the year *after* the conference: v205 (CoRL 2022) is dated 2023 and v270 (CoRL 2024) is dated 2025. DBLP and the reviewers use the conference year. I used the conference year throughout, with the PMLR volume and pages. If the editor wants the PMLR print year instead, change PerAct and DayDreamer to 2023 and OpenVLA to 2025.
- **arXiv DOIs.** Conference papers without a publisher DOI end with their arXiv DataCite DOI (10.48550/arXiv.*), which satisfies the "DOI or arXiv ID for every reference" rule.
